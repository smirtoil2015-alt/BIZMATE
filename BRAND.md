# BIZMATE Brand System

## Brand promise
**BIZMATE — The Intelligent Operating System for Business**

BIZMATE should feel like a calm, high-trust command center: intelligent, premium, precise and built for serious business decisions.

## Logo
Primary mark: the geometric **B** inside a rounded square, with a subtle connected-node network suggesting business intelligence and a connected company.

Use the supplied SVG logo as the primary horizontal lockup. Use the icon-only mark for app icons, favicon and compact navigation.

Do not:
- distort or stretch the mark
- add random gradients or outlines
- place the logo over busy imagery
- alter the B geometry

## Typography
Primary typeface: **Inter**.

Use:
- 800–900 for BIZMATE wordmark and major display headings.
- 700–800 for buttons, KPI values and navigation emphasis.
- 400–600 for body and supporting UI text.
- Tight display tracking for hero headings; wider tracking for uppercase labels.

Fallback: `"Inter", "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif`.

## UI style
BIZMATE uses a **premium dark SaaS** visual language:

- generous whitespace
- rounded 12–22px surfaces
- subtle 1px borders
- restrained glass / blur only where it improves hierarchy
- minimal shadows, never heavy neumorphism
- high information density without visual clutter
- cyan/violet reserved primarily for intelligence and primary actions
- green, amber and red communicate state, not decoration

### Cards
Cards are dark surfaces with a subtle border and soft shadow. Avoid excessive gradients inside data-heavy regions.

### Buttons
Primary: brand gradient, dark text, bold weight.
Secondary: transparent/dark surface, border, light text.
Danger: red accent used sparingly.

### Data visualization
Prefer simple bars, lines, comparison cards and status pills. Keep the visual language consistent and accessible.

### AI surfaces
BIZMATE Intelligence uses the cyan → blue → violet spectrum with a subtle glow. AI-generated recommendations must visually separate from confirmed company facts.

### Status language
- Critical: red
- Warning: amber
- Healthy: green
- Informational / AI: cyan

## Layout
Desktop shell:
- 240–260px sidebar
- 1px divider
- fluid content area
- max content width around 1180–1280px for public pages

Responsive behavior:
- collapse sidebar on smaller screens
- maintain 16px minimum page gutters
- stack multi-column cards below 900px
- preserve large touch targets on mobile

## Product voice
BIZMATE communicates with clarity and confidence. Avoid hype inside the product. Prefer:

"3 customers need attention"

over:

"WOW! 3 amazing opportunities!"

AI should explain **what changed, why it matters, and what can be done next**.
